# Cloud Strategy, Proofs of Concept, and Compliance (CCA 610)

Strategy and evaluation deliverables from my UMGC CCA 610 (Cloud Services and Technologies) coursework for BallotOnline (coursework case-study company).

## AWS Proof of Concept — Amazon WorkLink
- Built a proof of concept for **Amazon WorkLink** delivered through **CloudFormation**
- Configured **AWS SSO with SAML 2.0 federation** and domain verification for identity integration

## Azure Proof of Concept
- Feasibility study structure: objectives, **success metrics**, risk analysis, and an explicit **go/no-go** decision framework

## Research and analysis papers
- **AWS overview** and **Azure overview** papers
- **Cloud Risk and Compliance Issues Analysis** — regulatory and compliance risk assessment for cloud adoption
- **Proposal for a Compliance Program** — program ownership (CIO role), responsibilities, and implementation structure
- **IT Business Requirements** — functional requirements across SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS delivery models (multi-tenancy, vendor-managed updates, integration on demand, configurability)
- **IT Infrastructure / Azure TCO summary** — economic comparison of on-premises servers vs. Azure

## Risk framework selection (related analysis)
- Evaluated candidate frameworks and selected the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework** together with the **Cloud Security Alliance (CSA)** guidance as the best fit for BallotOnline's cloud risk management

## Skills demonstrated
- Proof-of-concept design and evaluation
- Identity federation (SSO, SAML 2.0)
- Cloud compliance and risk analysis; framework selection (NIST CSF, CSA)
- Business requirements and TCO analysis
